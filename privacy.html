Privacy Policy

Effective 6 August 2026

  1. Overview

    This policy explains what data ChromaSwap (the "Application") processes, and how, when it runs on your computer. The Application is developed and distributed by an individual developer, not a company, and does not operate any servers that your data passes through.

  2. Information we do not collect

    The Application does not create user accounts, does not use analytics or telemetry software, does not use cookies or tracking pixels (including on this website), and does not collect any personal information on behalf of the developer. Nothing about your use of the Application or this website is sent back to the developer.

  3. Video and audio files

    Video and audio files you select are processed entirely on your own device using a local video-processing engine. They are never uploaded anywhere by the Application, except to TikTok itself, and only at the moment you explicitly choose to publish a specific video.

  4. TikTok account authorization data

    If you log in to TikTok, the Application stores the OAuth access token and refresh token that TikTok issues, used only to publish content on your behalf. These are kept locally on your device, in your operating system's credential store where available, and are never transmitted to the developer or any other party. TikTok's own handling of your account data is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy, not this one.

  5. TikTok developer credentials

    If you configure your own TikTok developer app to use with the Application, your Client Key and Client Secret are likewise stored locally on your device only.

  6. How this data is used

    Locally stored TikTok credentials are used for exactly one purpose: authenticating requests to TikTok's API when you explicitly initiate an action, such as logging in or publishing a video. They are not used for any other purpose, and are not read or processed by anyone other than you and, transiently, TikTok's own servers when a request is made.

  7. Data sharing with third parties

    The only third party the Application communicates with is TikTok, via its official public API, and only for actions you explicitly initiate. The developer does not sell, rent, or share any data with advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party, because the developer does not receive or hold any of your data in the first place.

  8. Data security

    TikTok credentials are stored using your operating system's credential store (such as Windows Credential Locker) where available, the same mechanism used by many other desktop applications to store sensitive tokens securely. If that mechanism is unavailable, credentials are stored in a local file on your device instead.

  9. Data retention and deletion

    All data the Application stores — video files, output files, and TikTok credentials — lives on your device, under your control. Logging out of TikTok within the Application, or uninstalling it, removes the locally stored TikTok credentials. Deleting your video files through Windows removes them the same way it would for any other file.

  10. Your rights

    Because your data is stored only on your own device, you already have full access to it and full control over deleting it at any time — there is no separate request process needed, unlike with a hosted service. For data TikTok holds about your account, refer to TikTok's own privacy policy and account settings.

  11. Cookies and tracking on this website

    This website does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or any tracking technology. It consists of static pages only.

  12. Children's privacy

    The Application is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age required to hold a TikTok account.

  13. International users

    Because the Application processes data locally and does not transmit it to the developer, your data does not cross international borders as a result of using the Application, regardless of where you are located.

  14. Changes to this policy

    This policy may be revised from time to time. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Application after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

  15. Contact

    Questions about this policy can be sent to kyoungminjun@gmail.com.
