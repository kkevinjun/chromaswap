Personal desktop application for Windows

Swap your video's background without a green-screen studio.

ChromaSwap is a desktop app that takes a video recorded on a plain green background and layers new footage in behind it — no studio, no professional lighting rig, just two video files and a few settings.

What it does

  • Chroma-key compositing. Removes a green (or any chosen color) background from your video and replaces it with separate background footage, entirely on your own computer.
  • Automatic length matching. The combined video is trimmed to match your foreground clip's length, and the background footage is cut to the exact portion used.
  • Batch generation. Make several output videos in one run, reusing your foreground clip against fresh, non-overlapping stretches of background footage each time.
  • Flexible framing. Choose whether background footage is cropped to fill the frame, stretched to fit exactly, or cropped to a centered square with letterboxing.
  • Optional TikTok publishing. Once a video is ready, you can log in with your own TikTok account and publish it directly, using TikTok's official Content Posting API — your TikTok password is never entered into the app.
  • Runs locally. No account, no server, no analytics. Your video files never leave your computer unless you explicitly choose to publish one.

How it works

  1. Record your foreground clip on a plain background

    A green background works best. This clip carries your subject, any on-screen text, and the audio track that will be kept in the final video.

  2. Add background footage

    Any video works — this is what will show through once the green is removed. Its own audio is discarded; only the foreground clip's audio is kept.

  3. Adjust the key settings if needed

    Fine-tune the background color, key sensitivity, and edge blending so the cutout looks clean, then choose how the background footage should fill the frame.

  4. Generate — and optionally publish

    ChromaSwap composites the video locally and saves it to a folder you choose. If you've connected a TikTok account, you can publish it straight from the app.

Privacy by construction: ChromaSwap has no server component. Every video is processed on your own machine, and the only outside connection it ever makes is to TikTok's official API, and only when you explicitly choose to log in or publish. See the Privacy Policy for details.
